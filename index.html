Technological infrastructure behind MetricPoint

🛰️ SAR Edaphic Analysis (Sentinel-1)

Our platform injects raw C-band microwave data from the European Space Agency. Unlike optical images, radar penetrates vegetation cover and clouds, allowing us to calculate the real volumetric soil moisture from the first centimeters to the deep root zone, detecting stress before the plant shows visual symptoms.

📡 Live SINARAME Integration

Direct real-time connection with the weather radar network of the National Meteorological Service (SMN). We mathematically clean the reflectivity (dBZ) by cutting garbage visual vectors to overlay millimeter storm echoes over your lots. Anticipate hail fronts and severe convective storms minutes before they occur.

🧊 Topographic Digital Twins

We transform the view of your fields from 2D planes to massive 3D blocks by extracting Digital Elevation Models (DEM). Our graphics engine allows you to take "spatial X-rays" of the terrain at different depths, interacting with heat maps and discovering endorheic basins, runoff routes and areas prone to wilting or chronic waterlogging.
